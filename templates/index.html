Our Clergy & Community

Our dedicated priests and faithful community come together to celebrate the Holy Mass in Tamil, preserving our rich Catholic traditions while serving God in Kansas City. We are blessed to have clergy who understand our culture and language.

Community Celebrations

Our vibrant Tamil Catholic community comes together throughout the year to celebrate our faith, preserve our cultural heritage, and support one another in our spiritual journey.
